Designing Your World

  1. 01

    Start with the People.

    Every touchpoint has to serve its audience and the people using it to reach them. So I start by talking to both, learning what they need, and designing around how the work will be used and received.

  2. 02

    Build the World.

    I build one consistent world for a brand's story, across every medium. Because I work in all of them, I design each piece for where it's going next. When I direct a photoshoot, I already know which shots will become cutouts, product photos, or brochure layouts, and I frame and light them for it.

  3. 03

    Build the System.

    At scale, no one person should make every piece. So I create reusable assets, templates, and standards that keep a whole team consistent and the world intact, no matter who's building it.
